Branch Hours & Shifts

Type each branch's opening time and closing time yourself, with AM or PM (for example 10:00 AM and 3:00 AM). The closing time can be on the next calendar day.

  • While the shop is open, a report belongs to the day that is running. A report at 2 AM still counts for the day that opened at 10:00 AM the day before.
  • After the shop closes and before it opens again, the new day starts: the report counts for the next day. The waiter first gets a "late submission — are you sure?" message, and after OK the report shows Late submission on every dashboard.
  • 12:00 AM → 11:59 PM means an ordinary calendar day (never closed, never late). Waiters see their branch's opening and closing time on their page.

Shifts / day is how many shifts the branch runs. Audit can close the day (enter the day's Foodics) once all of these shifts have been submitted and approved.

Type it as 10 am, 10:30 pm, 3am — or in 24-hour style (22:30). A number with no AM/PM is read as 24-hour. It is tidied up when you leave the box.
